Privacy Policy — Transcript & Notes for YouTube™

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Transcript & Notes for YouTube™ ("the extension") does not collect personal data and never sells data.

  • The extension runs only on youtube.com pages. It reads the transcript that YouTube provides for the video you are watching and shows it in a panel on the page.
  • Transcripts are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Panel settings (for example, whether the panel is collapsed) are stored locally with Chrome's storage API on your device.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (since version 0.1.2): to learn which features are useful, the extension sends us the names of the panel buttons you use (for example "copy", "download .srt", "summarize with ChatGPT", "transcript loaded / not available"), the extension version, your browser language and a random ID created at installation. We do not send video IDs or titles, transcript text, search terms, your YouTube account, or anything you type. Our server (transcriptnote.com, hosted on Cloudflare) does not store your IP address; it keeps only the country derived from it. The data is used only to improve the extension, is never sold or shared, and is deleted after 13 months.
  • You can turn the statistics off at any time: panel menu (⋯) → uncheck "Share anonymous usage stats". After that nothing is sent.
  • The "Summarize with ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini" buttons copy a prompt with the transcript to your clipboard and open the chosen service in a new tab. Nothing is sent until you paste it yourself; that service's own privacy policy applies to what you send there.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no selling of data.

If this policy changes (for example, when optional AI features are added), this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Questions: use the "Support" tab on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
